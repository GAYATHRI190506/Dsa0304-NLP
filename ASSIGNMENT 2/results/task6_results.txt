6. INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The corpus contains public-transport enquiry language, so nouns, verbs, prepositions, determiners and location names occur frequently.

Proper nouns are used for city and location names. Numbers and time expressions are mapped to NUM/CD. Question words are separated into WHPRON and WHADV.

Ambiguity occurs when the same word can perform different grammatical functions depending on context. Examples include words such as 'book', 'train', 'return' and 'can'.

Important note: the corpus is synthetic. The annotation script provides an annotation aid and a simulated independent second annotation pass. For a strict manual annotation claim, the generated annotation file should be reviewed by the student/annotator before submission.
