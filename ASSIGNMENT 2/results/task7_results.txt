======================================================================

1. APPROACH
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The rule-based tagger uses a lexical dictionary followed by ordered linguistic rules. The rules examine punctuation, numbers, time expressions, proper nouns, function words, suffix patterns and finally unknown words.

7. INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The rule-based tagger performs well on the controlled public-transport vocabulary because many words have stable grammatical roles. Dictionary rules are particularly useful for city names, transport nouns, function words and modal auxiliaries.

Suffix-based rules provide a fallback for unseen words. However, purely lexical rules have difficulty when a word can have more than one grammatical role depending on context. This motivates the statistical HMM tagger in Task 8.
