RECOMMENDATION
==============

The recommended architecture for the university helpdesk is Earley parsing combined with feature structures and PCFG/Viterbi ranking.

Earley provides broad CFG coverage, recursion handling and ambiguity support. Feature structures add linguistic constraints such as agreement and subcategorization. PCFG/Viterbi ranking can then select the most probable interpretation among multiple valid parses.
